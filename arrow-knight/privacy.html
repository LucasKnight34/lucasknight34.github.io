Privacy Policy

Arrow Knight for iPhone. Effective October 14, 2026.

In short: Arrow Knight has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some data to show, measure, and protect those ads. You choose whether ads may be personalized and whether the game may track you. Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and between level ads.

Who we are

Arrow Knight is made by Lucas Knight ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to knightgames.support@gmail.com.

Data the game keeps on your device

The game saves the following only on your iPhone, so it can remember where you are:

  • Level progress, stars, coins, hints, daily challenge streak, and themes you own
  • Your sound, music, and haptics settings
  • Which in app purchases you own, so the game opens instantly (Apple remains the source of truth)

We never receive this data. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is kept in your iCloud account so your progress follows you to your other devices; Apple stores it under its own privacy policy, and we cannot see it. You can turn this off for the game in iOS Settings > your name > iCloud. Deleting the app deletes the copy on the device.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, the game schedules notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else, and you can turn them off in the game's Settings at any time.

Play data (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To balance the game, it sends anonymous play data to Google Analytics for Firebase: which levels you play, how long they take, whether you win, how you use hints, coins, and the shop, and which looks you wear, along with your device type, iOS version, rough location from your IP address, and a random id for this installation. It never includes your name, email, Game Center details, or anything you write, it does not use your device's advertising identifier, and it is never used for ads. You can turn it off in the game's Settings > Share play data. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising by Google AdMob

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and the Google User Messaging Platform. When ads load, Google may collect:

Google is an independent controller of this data. Learn how Google uses it at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices inside the game.
  • US state privacy rights. Where applicable, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information through the same Privacy choices screen.
  • Tracking. The game asks once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether it may track you. If you decline, the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on tracking. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Remove Ads. This purchase turns off banner and between level ads. Reward videos remain available and are always optional.
  • Reward videos are optional. The game never shows one unless you tap a button that offers it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports your achievement progress to Apple's Game Center so you can see it there, and posts your Daily Trial and Stakes Trial times to Game Center leaderboards, where other players can see them with your Game Center name. This is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store your Game Center identity.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple Account, or payment details. Apple tells the game only which products you own.

Children

Arrow Knight is a general audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention and deletion

Game data lives on your device until you delete the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies. You can reset your Advertising Identifier, or stop tracking, in iOS Settings at any time.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are best sent to Google, and we will gladly help you with them. Contact knightgames.support@gmail.com.

Security

Data sent by the Google SDKs is encrypted in transit. The game itself sends nothing to us. If you choose to email us (from Settings, or with feedback from a TestFlight test build), we receive what you write and send, and use it only to answer you and improve the game.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
