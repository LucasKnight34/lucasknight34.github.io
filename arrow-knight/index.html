Arrow Knight

A calm logic puzzle for iPhone. Slide every arrow off the board.

Need help? Email knightgames.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if something is not working, and we will reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

How do I play? Tap an arrow and it slides off the board in the direction it points. If something is in the way, it bumps back and costs a heart. Clear every arrow to win.

I bought something. How do I get it on a new iPhone? Open Settings in the game, or the Shop, and tap Restore purchases. Consumables such as hints and coins cannot be restored once used.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone? Yes. When you are signed in to iCloud, your progress, coins, and themes sync between your devices automatically.

How do I turn daily reminders on or off? In the game, open Settings > Daily reminder.

How do I change my ad privacy choices? In the game, open Settings > Privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Tracking can be changed in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Can I turn off ads? Yes. Remove Ads (or the Starter Bundle) turns off banner and between level ads for good. Reward videos stay available but are always optional.
